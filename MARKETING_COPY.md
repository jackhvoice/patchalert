# Launch copy for PatchAlert

Use these as starting drafts, not final copy — tweak the trade names, area,
and tone to sound like you rather than a marketer. Posted-by-a-real-person
framing consistently outperforms anything that reads like an ad in these
groups.

## Trade Facebook group / local builder forum post

> Built a little tool for myself and thought some of you might find it
> useful too. Every week I get an email listing new planning applications
> near [your area] for extensions, loft conversions, and garage
> conversions — basically a heads-up on jobs before the homeowner's even
> thought about getting quotes in. It's free to try, no account needed to
> see a sample: [link]. Happy to tweak it if people want different trade
> types covered.

## Landlord/property forum post (for the sister compliance product, once ready)

> Kept missing gas safety renewal dates across a few properties so I built
> a simple tracker that flags anything coming due and spits out a
> ready-made compliance pack PDF. Sharing in case it saves anyone else the
> same headache: [link].

## Short elevator pitch (for cold conversations, e.g. at a trade counter or builders' merchant)

> It's a simple email alert — you tell it your patch and what kind of jobs
> you go for, and it tells you when someone nearby applies for planning
> permission for that kind of work. So you can get in touch before
> they've even started getting quotes.

## Early-access / pre-payment framing

Since Stripe isn't wired up live yet, launch as a free early-access beta
rather than waiting for billing to be ready:

> Currently free while it's in early access — I'm looking for a handful of
> people to try it and tell me what's useful and what's missing before I
> start charging for it.

This does two things: gets real usage and feedback before you've invested
in a payment setup, and gives you a natural list of warm leads to convert
to paying customers the moment billing is live (a follow-up email like
"thanks for being an early user — from March it'll be £9/month, you can
lock in £6/month for life if you subscribe this week" tends to convert
well with people who've already gotten value from something free).

## Where to post

- Local Facebook groups: search "[your town/region] builders", "[your
  town/region] tradesmen", or general trade groups.
- Checkatrade / MyBuilder trade forums if they have a community section.
- r/UKConstruction or similar subreddits (check each community's
  self-promotion rules first — many require a "flair" or a specific day
  for this kind of post).
- Local WhatsApp/Facebook groups for specific trades you already have some
  connection to, even a loose one.
