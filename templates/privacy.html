Privacy Policy

Draft starting point — reviewed for plain-English clarity but not by a solicitor. Have this properly reviewed before you rely on it once PatchAlert has real paying customers, ideally alongside registering with the ICO if you don't have a data protection registration already.

What we collect

When you search on this site: the postcode and radius you enter, purely to run that one search — this isn't saved unless you subscribe. When you subscribe: your name, email address, postcode, search radius, and chosen trade keywords, so we can send your alerts.

Why we collect it

Solely to run the alert service you signed up for — matching new planning applications to your patch and emailing you when one comes up. We don't sell or share your details with advertisers.

Where the planning data comes from

Every planning application referenced in an alert is public record, published by the relevant local council and aggregated by UK PlanIt. We don't collect or infer anything about the applicant beyond what's already public on the planning register.

Other services we use

Your postcode is converted to a map location via postcodes.io (a free UK postcode lookup service) purely to run the search — no account or personal data is created there. Emails are sent via Resend, an email delivery service; your email address is passed to them only to deliver your alert. If you subscribe to a paid plan, your payment is handled by Stripe — we don't see or store your card details ourselves.

How long we keep it

For as long as you're subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we stop sending alerts immediately; your record is kept only so we don't accidentally re-add you later, but is no longer acted on. Email us if you'd like your data deleted entirely.

Your rights

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every alert email. Under UK GDPR you can also ask what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it entirely — just get in touch.
