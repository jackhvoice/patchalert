What this service is

PatchAlert emails you a digest of new UK planning applications matching a postcode, radius, and trade type you choose. All application data is public record, sourced via the UK PlanIt API and published by local councils. We don't guarantee completeness or accuracy of that underlying data, and we aren't affiliated with any council or planning authority.
